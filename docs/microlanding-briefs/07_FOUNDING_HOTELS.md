# ML-07 | Founding Hotels

## Status

`EXPERIMENT`

Special terms, cohort size and benefit package must be explicitly approved.

## 1. Audience

- high-fit hotels;
- responsive owner / GM;
- willingness to give feedback;
- willingness to test real workflows;
- potential pilot property;
- potential case study with future permission.

## 2. Goal

Create a small group of design partners instead of only collecting survey responses.

## 3. Primary offer

### Hero

**Станьте одним із перших готелів, які допоможуть формувати VDALO.**

Тестуйте продукт на реальних процесах, працюйте напряму з продуктовою командою та допомагайте визначати, що потрібно готелям у щоденній роботі.

**CTA:** `Подати заявку`

## 4. Potential benefits

Only activate approved items:

- priority onboarding;
- direct product-team contact;
- extended trial;
- special commercial terms;
- early access to selected capabilities;
- influence through structured feedback.

Do not promise implementation of requested features.

## 5. What we ask from hotel

- honest feedback;
- agreed research calls;
- bounded pilot workflow;
- access to operational context necessary for evaluation;
- structured feedback after usage.

Any customer-data access requires separate operational and legal process.

## 6. Page structure

1. Hero.
2. Why Founding Hotels exists.
3. Who is a fit.
4. What participants receive.
5. What VDALO expects.
6. Selection process.
7. FAQ.
8. Application.

## 7. Qualification form

- name;
- property;
- role;
- contact;
- property_type;
- room_count;
- current_PMS;
- main_problem;
- willingness_to_test;
- willingness_to_feedback.

## 8. Selection logic

Strong fit examples:

- clear repeated workflow;
- reachable decision-maker;
- willingness to run a real pilot;
- enough event volume;
- technically feasible stack;
- realistic onboarding/support load.

## 9. Post-submit

`application -> qualification -> interview -> selected / not selected -> pilot / trial -> review -> commercial`

## 10. Scarcity rule

Never write:

`Лише 10 місць`

unless the team has truly fixed and enforces a 10-property cohort.

## 11. Do not publish

- special price without approval;
- guaranteed roadmap influence;
- guaranteed feature requests;
- case/publicity rights without permission.
