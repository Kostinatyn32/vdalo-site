# ML-02 | VDALO Research Partner

## Status

`EXPERIMENT`

The 30-day benefit, interview duration and eligibility must be approved before launch.

## 1. Audience

- owner / GM willing to speak with product team;
- early adopter;
- hotelier with real operational context;
- potential design partner;
- participant from hotel communities.

## 2. Goal

Acquire 10-20 high-quality interviews and convert the strongest participants into real product users or design partners.

## 3. Primary offer

### Hero

**Допоможіть нам зробити VDALO кориснішим для реальної роботи українських готелів.**

Розкажіть, як сьогодні працює ваш готель, що команда робить вручну та якими системами користується.

**Test incentive:** `30 днів VDALO без оплати`

**CTA:** `Взяти участь у дослідженні`

Do not claim a limited number of places unless it is truly limited.

## 4. Value exchange

Hotel gives:

- 20-30 minute interview, exact duration to approve;
- honest workflow feedback;
- optional second feedback session after product usage.

VDALO gives:

- approved research incentive;
- access to product if technically and operationally ready;
- direct dialogue with product team.

## 5. Page structure

1. Hero.
2. Why research exists.
3. Who we are looking for.
4. What we ask.
5. What participant receives.
6. What happens after interview.
7. Privacy / recording explanation.
8. Form.
9. FAQ.

## 6. Research topics

Ask about:

- property type;
- room / unit count;
- current PMS;
- channels;
- last concrete operational incident;
- repeated manual work;
- systems involved;
- active staff time;
- current workaround;
- migration anxiety;
- what they already pay for;
- who approves software;
- willingness for real next step.

Avoid leading questions such as "How badly do you need automation?"

## 7. Form

Required:

- name;
- property;
- contact;
- role.

Recommended:

- room_count;
- current_PMS;
- biggest_manual_process;
- preferred_contact_time.

## 8. Post-submit

`form -> qualification -> interview booking -> interview -> incentive activation if approved -> usage feedback -> pilot / commercial / nurture`

## 9. Conversion goals

Primary:

- qualified research interview held.

Secondary:

- design partner;
- trial activation;
- pilot;
- paid.

## 10. Landing copy fragments

### Problem

**Ми не хочемо будувати продукт на припущеннях.**

Нам важливо побачити, як реально працюють бронювання, рецепція, комунікація, housekeeping та внутрішня координація.

### CTA block

**Готові показати, як працює ваш готель сьогодні?**

`Взяти участь у дослідженні`

## 11. Do not publish without approval

- "30 днів безкоштовно" as permanent offer;
- exact interview duration;
- guaranteed product access;
- fake participant count;
- "ваш feedback точно буде реалізовано".
