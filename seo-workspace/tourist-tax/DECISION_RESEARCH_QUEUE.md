## Source handling

- Tax Service regional pages summarize IAC LOGICA data as of 2026-01-30. Use them to discover and cross-check local rules, not as a substitute for the council decision and its amendments.
- An absent community entry in the Tax Service summary does not mean a zero rate or exemption.
- Workbook rates remain candidate values, separate from verified rates, and must not enter the live calculator without primary-source confirmation.
- Current queue as of 2026-10-14: 13 first-wave city pages pass local publication gates and 17 remain blocked. Cherkasy's signed 2026 Tax Service Form 6 and the city council's 2026–2028 budget forecast confirm 0.50% domestic and 5.00% inbound; its calculator is year-scoped and city/community codes match KATOTTG. The readable contents of 2023 amendment №51-32 remain unavailable, so the page does not claim an exact restoration date. Kolomyia's page uses the council's 2021 rule effective from 2022, corroborated by the 2025 signed Tax Service form and 2026 council budget forecast; no standalone 2026 Tax Service rate form was found. Ivano-Frankivsk's decision №140-43, KATOTTG codes, rates, 2026 Tax Service form, example arithmetic and unique page copy have been reviewed and tested. Ternopil uses a tested price-banded calculator and refuses a result at the overlapping UAH 320 boundary. Zaporizhzhia uses tested price bands with unambiguous endpoints at UAH 300 and UAH 600. Lviv is locally available from its signed 2026 Tax Service schedule, with unresolved exact boundaries disclosed and calculator requests at UAH 750 and UAH 1,200 rejected. Uzhhorod's form has an internal inconsistency and remains blocked; Bar needs further decision/amendment review; Odesa and Kyiv remain blocked on legal period/full-act review; 261 communities await primary decision research.
- Article 268 of the Tax Code requires the applicable local council decision and correct place/community match. Order №376 is maintained as a separate territorial reference and is not used as a universal tourist-tax rate or calculator gate.
